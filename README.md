# C2_UI
C2_UI is a web-based command-and-control interface that allows you to track and automate devices such as drones.

## Devices
Devices such as drones are at the core of C2_UI. After a Device has been registered, its status (e.g. battery, location) can be tracked through the interface.

## Routes
Routes are pre-defined paths which Devices can follow on Missions. Routes can be created by marking multiple waypoints for a Device to navigate to and from.

## Missions
Once you have Devices registered and Routes created, you can then assemble them into Missions. Before a Mission begins, devices are assigned to a Route to follow. After starting a Mission, all Devices will begin their Routes simultaneously.
